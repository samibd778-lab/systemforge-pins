Study tips

5 study methods that actually work

1
Active recallClose the book, write what you remember, then check.
2
Spaced reviewsReview a topic over days and weeks, not 5 times in one night.
3
Pomodoro25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes of break.
4
Practice problemsWithout the solution, with a timer.
5
Teach itExplain it in simple words. Where you get stuck is what to study next.
