Habit reset

The 30-day habit reset plan

1
Week 1 · Days 1–7Only the tiny versions. Don’t add new habits.
2
Week 2 · Days 8–14Grow 1 or 2 habits a little. Aim for 4 out of 6.
3
Week 3 · Days 15–21Keep the rhythm. Try one perfect day.
4
Week 4 · Days 22–30Change the habit that isn’t working. Plan next month.
Progress, not perfection. A 4 out of 6 day is a good day.

Habit tips

Missed a day? Do this, not that

“I forgot”
  • Move the cue where you can’t miss it
  • Set one phone reminder
“I was too tired”
  • Do the tiny version only
  • Move the habit earlier
“I got bored”
  • Change the place or the music
  • Raise the goal a little
“I missed a week”
  • Don’t start from zero
  • Pick 3 habits, not 6, for a while
Never miss twice. One missed day is normal.

Budgeting tips

The 50/30/20 budget on $3,000 a month

50% · Needs
  • About $1,500
  • Rent, food, bills, transport
30% · Wants
  • About $900
  • Fun, eating out, hobbies
20% · Savings & debt
  • About $600
  • Safety cushion first
Rent is high?
  • Try 60/20/20
  • The best budget is the one you keep
Simple method, not financial advice.
