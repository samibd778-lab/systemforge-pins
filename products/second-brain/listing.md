Your whole life in one calm Notion page.

Second Brain OS is a ready-to-use Notion system built on the PARA method (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive). Capture ideas, plan your goals, track habits and look back on your weeks, all from one home page.

WHAT'S INSIDE

🏠 Home dashboard
- Today widget: clock, day/week/month/year progress, mini calendar and a thought for the day
- Menu on the left with every page and database one click away

🎯 Goals
- Big goals for this year or quarter, with "why it matters"
- Progress bar that fills up as linked projects are done

🚀 Projects + ✅ Tasks
- Project board: Someday, Next up, In progress, On hold, Done
- Progress bar from ticked tasks + days left before the deadline
- Next 7 days list and a calendar of open tasks

🗒️ Notes + ⚡ Quick capture
- Inbox: new notes wait here until you give them a home
- Key takeaway on every note, favorites on the home page
- Quick capture form for saving ideas fast

🧭 Areas + 📚 Resources
- Areas of life with a "what good looks like" line, their projects, notes and resources
- Resources for topics, courses, podcasts and tools

✔️ Habits
- One row per day, 5 habits you can rename, score ●●●○○ for each day
- This week view on the home page

📓 Journal
- 2-minute daily check-in: mood, energy, one win, one thing you're grateful for
- Morning and evening questions, mood board for the month

📖 Reading list
- Bookshelf with progress bar, rating and the one key idea from each book
- Link your notes to the book they came from

🔁 Reviews
- Weekly review checklist + a Weekly reviews log with ratings
- Monthly review and Yearly review pages

📱 Mobile home
- A light page for your phone: capture, next 7 days, habits and journal

📘 Guides
- Start here (5 minutes), How PARA works, How to take good notes, 30 things worth capturing, FAQ

🎁 BONUS: Notion for beginners (PDF, 7 pages)
Set up, the 6 things to know, databases made simple, your routine, shortcuts and fixes.

Example data is included so you can see how everything links together. Delete it when you're ready.
