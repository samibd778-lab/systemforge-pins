## What it tells us
- **"aesthetic"** comes 2nd or 3rd for almost every topic: titles and images should say and look aesthetic.
- **"ideas"** and **"template"** are top modifiers: board names "X Ideas" are right.
- **"notion template ideas personal"** is a real search: our life templates fit it.
- Excel / Google Sheets / Goodnotes / Canva are other tools: never use them (not true for our products).
